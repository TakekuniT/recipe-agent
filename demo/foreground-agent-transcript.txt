## **Pad Thai** (Allrecipes)
⭐ **Rating:** 3.9/5 (771 reviews)
⏱️ **Total Time:** 45 minutes (10 min prep, 15 min cook)
🍽️ **Servings:** 6

### **Description:**
This simple pad thai recipe with chicken uses rice noodles and has a delicious sauce made with vinegar, sugar, fish sauce, and red pepper.

### **Ingredients:**
- 1 (12 ounce) package rice noodles
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breast halves, cut into bite-sized pieces
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- 4 eggs
- 3 tablespoons white sugar
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar
- ⅛ tablespoon crushed red pepper
- 2 cups bean sprouts
- 3 green onions, chopped
- ¼ cup crushed peanuts
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

### **Instructions:**
1. Soak rice noodles in cold water until soft, 30 to 50 minutes. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat butter in a wok; add chicken and sauté until browned. Remove chicken and set aside.
3. Heat oil in the wok over medium-high heat. Crack eggs into hot oil and cook until firm. Stir in chicken and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add softened noodles, sugar, fish sauce, vinegar, and red pepper; mix well until noodles are tender. Adjust seasonings to taste.
5. Stir bean sprouts into wok and cook for 3 minutes.
6. Serve topped with green onions, crushed peanuts, and a wedge of lemon.

### **Nutrition (per serving):**
- Calories: 524 kcal
- Protein: 26 g
- Carbohydrates: 59 g
- Fat: 21 g
